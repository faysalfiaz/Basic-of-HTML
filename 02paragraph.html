paragraph Tag

A paragraph is a self-contained unit of discourse, consisting of one or more sentences that support a single, central idea or topic. Typically 4-7 sentences long,
it functions as a, section, clause, passage, or component of a larger text. Structured with a topic sentence, supporting details, and a concluding sentence, it organizes ideas logically
